The evolution of the wear sleeve

The DuraSleeve Story

The same problem-solving lineage that created the original wear sleeve led to DuraSleeve — a hard-chrome evolution built to outlast it.

1

The invention — late 1960s

The original Speedi-Sleeve was invented by an engineer in Burnaby, British Columbia, and trademarked in 1968 by International Speedi-Sleeve Ltd. It solved a real problem: a thin sleeve worn over a damaged shaft so the same seal size could be reused.

2

The sale — a growing brand

The rights were transferred to Chicago Rawhide (CR), a Chicago-area sealing manufacturer. The record of the sale and trademark ownership is documented in the public trademark register.

3

From CR to SKF

SKF acquired Chicago Rawhide in 1990, and the Speedi-Sleeve name carried on inside the SKF product line. Today it is known and sold as SKF Speedi-Sleeve.

4

The evolution — DuraSleeve

The original Speedi-Sleeve could be improved upon. Its creator went on to develop DuraSleeve: a hard-chrome-surface, stainless-steel repair sleeve in the same family of products, engineered for a longer, more durable repair.

5

Built and proven today

DuraSleeve is a thin-walled, press-fit shaft repair sleeve for worn or damaged shaft seal surfaces — designed and manufactured in North America, with the durability to outlast.
