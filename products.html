Shaft repair sleeves

Find your DuraSleeve

Thin-walled, press-fit repair sleeves for worn or damaged shaft seal surfaces. SAE/ANSI 304 stainless steel with a hard chrome surface. Use the same seal size.

Step 1 — measure

Measure the shaft, not the groove

Measure the shaft in three places and use the average. That average diameter is the number you match to the sizing chart.

1

Wrap a caliper or mic

Measure at the unworn part of the shaft seal journal.

2

Do it in three spots

Take three readings around the shaft and average them.

3

Match it below

Enter your average diameter and we'll show matching part numbers.

Step 2 — match

Sizing lookup (imperial)

Type your measured shaft diameter in inches. We'll find the DuraSleeve part numbers whose range covers it.

Use the average of three readings. Values in fractional inches work too — e.g. 1-3/8 → 1.375.
Why DuraSleeve

Built to last the repair

Hard chrome surface

Around 68 Rockwell C, supporting wear resistance, low friction, and corrosion resistance. The surface finish is the whole point.

Stainless steel

SAE/ANSI 304 stainless steel core. Strong, corrosion-resistant, and machinable for the repair fit.

Interference fit

Designed as a press fit / interference fit. It should not slide on freely. Don't use heat to install it.

Same seal size

Install over the worn surface and reuse the original seal size — no shaft replacement, less downtime.
