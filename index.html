Good to know

Common questions

What is a DuraSleeve?
A thin-walled shaft repair sleeve designed to install over a worn or damaged shaft seal surface while using the same seal size.
How do I know which size to use?
Measure the shaft in three places and use the average. Then match it to the sizing chart range for the part you need.
Can I heat it to install?
No. DuraSleeve is designed as a press-fit / interference fit. The installation guide says not to use heat during installation.
What about splines, keyways, threads or cross holes?
The installation guide warns against installing over splines, keyways, threads, or cross holes. Check the guide for application limits.
Does it really last longer?
In DuraSleeve's own real-world testing against a legacy sleeve, DuraSleeve lasted at least 2x longer. See the proof page for the documented side-by-side test.
