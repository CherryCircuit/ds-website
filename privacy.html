Privacy Policy

How Dura Sleeve Inc. handles the information you share with us.

Last updated: 2026

Who we are

Dura Sleeve Inc. ("DuraSleeve", "we", "us") manufactures and sells hard-chrome shaft repair sleeves. Our website and the repair contest are operated by Dura Sleeve Inc., Abbotsford, British Columbia, Canada.

What we collect

When you use our site, contact us, or enter our repair contest, we may collect:

  • Contact information — your email address, name, and any details you provide.
  • Your submission — a photo and any comments you supply with a repair story or contest entry.
  • Technical information — standard analytics such as browser type and pages visited, used to understand how the site is used.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your inquiries and requests.
  • To administer the repair contest and contact winners.
  • To improve our website, products, and services.
  • To share repair photos publicly (on our site and social channels) to celebrate repairs, as agreed when you submit.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information. We only use your email to respond to you or to contact you about the contest or an inquiry.

Contest submissions

By entering the repair contest, you confirm you have the right to the photo you submit and agree that we may share it publicly to showcase the repair. We store your contact details only as needed to run the contest and contact a potential winner.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you submit. While no method of transmission is 100% secure, we use appropriate safeguards for the data we hold.

Third parties

We may use trusted third-party services (such as hosting and analytics providers) to operate the website. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security commitments.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at sales@durasleeve.com or 1-866-365-1279.
