How a Wear Sleeve Reduces Downtime

Downtime costs far more than parts. When a shaft seal surface fails, the fastest way back online is usually a repair sleeve. ## The problem with shaft replacement Replacing a shaft usually means disassembly, machining, cylindrical grinding, and possibly a long wait for a part that may not even be in stock. Every one of those hours is downtime. ## The sleeve fix A wear sleeve installs over the worn shaft surface in minutes. You clean the shaft, measure, match the size, and tap it into place with the included tool. No machining. No grinding. The same seal fits. ## Less downtime, less cascading damage A leaking seal lets contamination in and lubrication out, which cascades into bearing and component failure. Fixing the seal surface quickly stops that cascade. ## Choose a durable sleeve A sleeve that wears out again means the downtime happens twice. DuraSleeve's hard chrome surface is around 68 Rockwell C, and in its own real-world testing DuraSleeve lasted at least 2x longer than a legacy sleeve. That's fewer repairs and fewer shutdowns. **The bottom line:** a wear sleeve gets equipment running fast because it installs in minutes with no machining. And a durable sleeve means you stay running.
