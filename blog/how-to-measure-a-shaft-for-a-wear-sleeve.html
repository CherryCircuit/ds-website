How to Measure a Shaft for a Wear Sleeve

Measuring a shaft the right way is the difference between a sleeve that fits and one that doesn't. Here's how to do it. ## Clean the shaft first Wipe the shaft clean and remove any burrs or raised metal. A clean, smooth reading gives you a reliable measurement. ## Measure in three places Take measurements around the shaft at the seal journal. Worn shafts aren't perfectly round — three readings give you the true average. Use a caliper or micrometer. ## Use the average Add the three readings and divide by three. That average is the diameter you match to the sizing chart. The sleeve needs to press into the surface with an interference fit. ## Match the range Every sleeve has a shaft diameter range. Pick the sleeve whose range covers your average measurement. If you're between sizes, check the chart carefully. ## Don't slide it on A shaft repair sleeve is a press fit / interference fit. It should not slide on freely. If it does, re-check your measurement. **The bottom line:** measure clean, take three readings, use the average, and match it to the sizing chart. That's how you get a reliable repair.
