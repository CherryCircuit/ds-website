What Is a Shaft Repair Sleeve?

A shaft repair sleeve, also called a wear sleeve, is a thin-walled sleeve that installs over a worn or damaged shaft seal surface. When a shaft seal surface gets scored or worn, the seal can no longer hold. Instead of replacing the shaft, you press-on a sleeve that gives the seal a fresh counterface again. ## Why shafts wear The shaft seal surface is one of the hardest-working, most exposed parts of a rotating machine. Over time, dirt, grit and the seal lip itself wear a groove into the shaft. Once that groove forms, the seal leaks, lubricant escapes and contamination gets in. ## Why a sleeve works The whole point of a wear sleeve is that it restores the shaft to its original seal diameter without an expensive repair. Because it's thin-walled, you use the same seal size you already have. No machining. No shaft replacement.
